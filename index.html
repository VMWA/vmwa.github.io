How I work

Two ways to work together: a scoped project with a clear end, or a monthly retainer for ongoing capacity. Short path from idea to production. Primarily remote.

Project work

A clear goal with a clear end: an MVP, an internal tool, or a defined piece of a product. I scope it, build it, and hand over something that runs in production.

Ongoing / retainer

A set amount of time each month for features, fixes, and whatever comes up. Steady capacity without a full-time hire, with room to shift when priorities change.

I use AI tools to move faster. I still direct the architecture, weigh design tradeoffs, and review what goes to production.
